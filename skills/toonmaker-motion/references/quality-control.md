# Quality Control Checklist

## Educational and factual quality

- [ ] The video has one clear learning outcome.
- [ ] The opening makes the topic and reason to watch clear.
- [ ] Explanations follow a logical order.
- [ ] Technical terms are defined when needed.
- [ ] Examples actually support the point being taught.
- [ ] Numbers, quotations, and factual claims are supported by the available source basis.
- [ ] No invented study, statistic, citation, or attribution appears.
- [ ] The conclusion reinforces the central takeaway.

## Script and pacing

- [ ] The script exists before animation implementation.
- [ ] Scene timings cover the full duration.
- [ ] Narration fits the allotted time at a natural speaking pace.
- [ ] On-screen text is concise and not a duplicate transcript.
- [ ] Each scene has one primary communication goal.
- [ ] Pauses are allowed for complex visuals.

## Visual direction

- [ ] UI-style panels, cards, diagrams, labels, and typography are the primary visual language.
- [ ] Cartoon styling comes from graphic design and illustration accents.
- [ ] Any characters remain secondary and have a clear purpose.
- [ ] The video does not drift into a character-led cartoon film.
- [ ] Layout hierarchy and alignment are consistent.
- [ ] Text is readable at the final display size.
- [ ] Contrast is sufficient, and color is not the only carrier of meaning.
- [ ] No visual element blocks important labels or captions.

## Animation and rendering

- [ ] Every scene can render at an arbitrary timestamp.
- [ ] The same timestamp produces the same frame.
- [ ] Fonts and assets are loaded before capture.
- [ ] No unintended blank frames, flicker, clipping, or overflow appears.
- [ ] Transitions are deliberate and do not hide information.
- [ ] The video has the requested dimensions and frame rate.
- [ ] The final encoded file plays and has the expected duration.

## Audio and captions

- [ ] Voiceover matches the script.
- [ ] Speech is clear and not masked by music.
- [ ] Sound effects are restrained and intentional.
- [ ] Audio and visuals are synchronized.
- [ ] Captions match the spoken words and are timed correctly when supplied.
- [ ] No audio ends abruptly unless intentionally designed.

## Delivery

- [ ] Script, storyboard, source, and referenced assets are included as promised.
- [ ] The final video file exists and can be opened.
- [ ] README instructions match the actual project.
- [ ] Third-party attributions and licenses are included when required.
- [ ] The completion note distinguishes verified outputs from anything not completed.
